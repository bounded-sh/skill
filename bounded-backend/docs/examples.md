# Example policies

Complete policies indexed by intent. Each page is one self-contained `policy.json` plus operations and reasoning, behavior-tested end to end before shipping here.

| You are building | Open |
|---|---|
| A marketplace with listings, orders, and a per-buyer spend cap | [marketplace](examples/marketplace.md) |

| Anything moving funds onchain (escrow, vaults, treasury, staking, markets, tokens, NFTs, randomness, liquidity) | [onchain examples index](../../bounded-onchain/docs/examples.md) |

Recipes for individual rules (ownership, admin, membership, time windows, validation) are in the [access-pattern cookbook](access-patterns.md); one-screen syntax is the [policy cheat sheet](policy-cheat-sheet.md).
